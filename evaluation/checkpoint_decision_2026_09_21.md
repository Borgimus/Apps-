## Decision rule at five sessions and ten eligible opportunities

1. The checkpoint triggers a written review, never automatic reactivation.
   Current broker-entry permissions remain empty, ORB/VWAP remain shadow-only,
   and RSI remains diagnostic-only.
2. Audit quote provenance, entry identity, quantities, priced exits, missing
   paths and chronological portfolio admission before comparing P&L. Preserve
   documented health exceptions and show their sensitivity separately.
3. If baseline expectancy remains non-positive, or outcomes are unresolved,
   retain suspension. A positive tenth anchor is not grounds for activation.
4. Ten anchors are a research checkpoint, not sufficient live-readiness
   evidence. The previously discussed 20–30 fill-validated observations are an
   evidence-collection goal, not statistical proof or an automatic trading
   gate. Reactivation requires separate approval and a prospective, specified
   protocol with reconciled paper fills and realistic portfolio constraints.
5. Freeze current delta, DTE, premium budget, quality and regime rules during
   this cohort. If the review warrants a filter experiment, declare a new
   cohort and one specific change in advance; retain the unchanged comparator.
6. Keep inverted-direction outcomes diagnostic. They do not establish a
   tradable opposite-direction strategy without independently eligible entries,
   fresh prices, capacity checks and prospective confirmation.

## Funnel and experiment interpretation

September 18's 2,096 raw observations include 2,082 RSI diagnostics. The relevant
entry funnel is 14 entry-signal observations, three unique setups and one
eligible anchor. Raw polling observations are not independent opportunities.

Report unique setups, overlapping blocker combinations and sole initial
blockers. A sole blocker identifies a candidate for investigation, not the
expected result of a numerical threshold adjustment. A setup can become
eligible later, and removing one check does not prove a fill or portfolio slot.
No one-factor threshold simulation is claimed without the requisite rejected
contract and quote-path evidence.

If the checkpoint still contains no exit-trigger crossings, record the exit
comparison as inconclusive and preserve the fixed comparator. Any new threshold
or alternative exit study needs a prospective separate specification. Do not
select the best threshold retrospectively from these six paths.

Opportunity arrival is variable. At the observed 1.2 anchors per accepted
session, four additional sessions is only a rough projection for the next four
anchors; missed sessions, health exclusions and changing markets can extend it.
